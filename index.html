About

Lewis, founder

I photograph and film the days people want to keep, and I build the archive around them.

I'm Lewis, a creative director, photographer and filmmaker based in Phnom Penh. Lew's.Archive is my own practice: the person you message is the person who shoots your day.

I work on weddings and on commercial projects for brands, in Cambodia and abroad. When a day needs more hands, I bring a second shooter and an assistant I trust, and I direct the whole team myself.

Everything I deliver is treated as an archive: edited with care, labelled, and backed up for twelve months after delivery.

Process

From first message to archive

  1. Enquire

    Tell me the date, place and what you have in mind.

  2. Quote

    You receive a written quotation, valid for a set period.

  3. Sign

    Sign the service agreement and pay the 50% deposit to hold your date.

  4. Shoot

    I direct the day and the crew. You stay in the moment.

  5. Receive

    Photos in 3–4 weeks, films in 4–6. Balance due before delivery.

Questions

Terms in short

How do I hold my date?

Sign the service agreement and pay the 50% deposit. A date is not held until both are done.

Can we move the date?

Yes, once, at no charge and subject to availability. After that a fee may apply.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 30 days before the shoot, the deposit is refunded less any costs already spent. Within 30 days it is not refundable.

When do I get my files?

Photos within 3–4 weeks and films within 4–6 weeks of the event, as an online gallery or download link. Rush delivery is available.

Can I use the photos for my business?

Personal use, including sharing online with credit, is included. Commercial or advertising use is quoted separately.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel outside Phnom Penh is billed separately, and destination weddings start from $3,500 plus travel.

What if you cannot make it on the day?

If illness or an emergency stops me, I will find a suitable replacement. If I cannot, every payment is refunded in full.
